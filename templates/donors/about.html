About us

Blood when it’s needed, from people nearby.

A simple way to connect blood donors with patients who urgently need their type — in one place, in real time.

When a patient needs blood urgently, families end up calling around and posting in scattered group chats, and willing donors never know when they are needed. LifeDrop brings it into one place: post a request with the blood type and city, and matching donors can see it and offer to help right away.

Find fast

Search open requests by blood type and city, and see what’s needed closest to you.

Offer to donate

Reach out to a requester in one click and arrange to help — no middlemen.

Verified donors

Upload a donor card to earn a Verified badge that requesters can trust.

Our mission

To make sure no patient waits for blood because the right donor simply didn’t know. We’re a student team building technology that turns willing donors into timely help.
